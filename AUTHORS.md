# Authors

## Art

- **The loading screen**: by g0atbutt (`port/assets/loading`).

- **The game's icon** (the macOS app's, the Android app's launcher icon,
  `halo.exe`'s, the Linux release's and the desktop windows'): the OpenH1 logo, Tail Code, by Erin Cena
  (`port/assets/icon/openh1-mark.svg`, from openh1.com's
  `website/public/favicon.svg`).
- **Steam's library artwork** (the capsules, hero, logo and icon of the
  Steam shortcut the Linux and Steam Frame installs add, OpenH1's and
  OpenH1 VR's): the OpenH1 lockup and mark by Erin Cena
  (`website/public/brand`) in openh1.com's Boot Glow look, drawn by
  `tools/steam_art.py` from `port/assets/steam/art.html`.
- **Discord's Rich Presence art** (`port/assets/discord`: `logo`, the
  game's icon's OpenH1 mark (above) on a dark tile; `party` and `host`,
  drawn for OpenH1 in openh1.com's Boot Glow colors).
- **The controller's A, B, X and Y buttons** (the menus' button icons, the
  HUD messages' and the Android touch controls'): redraws by saulob
  (`port/assets/buttons`).
- **The HUD's button styles** (Classic, Gems and Menu Gems, the black and
  white buttons, the triggers and the stick clicks, in the HUD and the
  Android touch controls): added by chestahh (OpenCE #380,
  `port/assets/buttons`, `tools/button_assets.py`); Classic's faces are
  Aerocatia's and Jessie's stencils (below) in its colours.
- **The high-res HUD's redraws** where Halo PC Restored has nothing larger
  than the Xbox's bitmap (the health meter's base, the motion sensor's
  blips and sweeper, and the outlines of the vehicles' and passengers'
  panels): by MrBruh (`port/assets/hud/svg`), based on reference photos by
  Aerocatia. The sniper rifle's zoom labels (2x, 10x) are set in Cyborg
  Interface Gothic (`port/assets/fonts`).
- **The high-res text's font**, Cyborg Interface Gothic (the menus' and
  the HUD's text, `port/assets/fonts`): by fizzy, made to match Halo's
  interface font, many of its glyphs derived from Overpass and the others
  drawn by them; under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
  (`CyborgInterfaceGothic-OFL.txt`). Taken from ChupathingyCE.
- **The corrected rocket launcher and sniper rifle reticles, and the
  sniper rifle's 2x and 10x** (`port/assets/hud/corrections`, packed by
  `tools/hud_corrections.py`): artwork supplied by OpenCE's project owner,
  its original artist unverified; fitted by chestahh and Erik (OpenCE).
- **The menus' mouse cursor** (the blue arrow the menus show for the
  mouse): by pugzysixtyfour (`port/assets/cursor`).

## Third-party art

- **The high-res HUD** (its meters, panels, counters, reticles, scopes,
  waypoints, message icons and their pictures of the weapons and the skull,
  the reticle's warnings, the multiplayer icons, and the classic style's
  controller buttons): Aerocatia's and Jessie's high-res HUD art from
  [Halo PC Restored](https://github.com/Aerocatia/halopc-restored) r220
  (`port/assets/hud/art`), used with Aerocatia's permission on the
  condition that Aerocatia and Jessie are credited; not under OpenH1's
  licence. See `port/assets/hud/NOTICE.md`. ChupathingyCE first fitted it
  to the Xbox maps' sheets; `tools/hud_assets.py` follows its way of
  cutting and placing it.
- **Voice chat's speaker icons** (who talks, and who is muted, in the lobby,
  on the scoreboard and in the game): `volume-2` and `volume-x` from
  [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) 1.53.0, copyright (c) 2026 Lucide Icons and
  Contributors, under the ISC licence
  (`port/assets/icons/lucide/LICENSE`).
- **The Mods menu's warning icons** (a mod out of its requested order, and
  a mod that cannot be played): `triangle-alert` and `circle-x` from
  [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) 1.53.0, under the same licence.
- **The server browsers' favorite star**: `star` from
  [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) 1.53.0, under the same licence.
- **The server browsers' search icons** (a column's search, and its
  clearing): `search` and `x` from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) 1.53.0,
  under the same licence.
- **The Android touch controls' line icons** (start, back, the D-pad, the
  toolbar): outlines of glyphs of [Mobile Controls](https://kenney.nl/assets/mobile-controls)
  1.0 by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), CC0
  (`port/assets/touch/kenney-mobile-controls`).
- **The additional controller button styles** (`port/assets/controller_buttons`,
  the Gamepads menu's button styles): supplied by chestahh (OpenCE), their
  original artist unverified.
