# The message icons' pictures: Aerocatia's and Jessie's art

The PNGs in `art/` are Aerocatia's and Jessie's high-resolution HUD art from
[Halo PC Restored](https://github.com/Aerocatia/halopc-restored), release
r220: sheets of the message icons in its tags
(`tags/ui/hud/bitmaps/combined/hud_msg_icons.bitmap`, bitmaps 0, 1, 3 and
4), decoded as they are, unchanged. `hud_msg_icons__0.png` to
`hud_msg_icons__3.png` in this folder are their pictures of the weapons and
the skull, cut from those sheets and placed, unscaled, where the Xbox maps'
sheets have them, by `tools/hud_assets.py` (pictures). The art is theirs.

It is not under OpenH1's licence. Its permission is as ChupathingyCE's
NOTICE reports it (`port/assets/hud/NOTICE.md` in
[ChupathingyCE/chupathingyce](https://github.com/ChupathingyCE/chupathingyce)
at 9e03ff5d), of the same art:

> They are not covered by Chupathingy's license. They are used with
> Aerocatia's permission, given on October 14, 2026 ("it was released to be
> used"), on the condition that Aerocatia and Jessie are credited. Anyone
> reusing them should follow the same terms, or ask Aerocatia.

The art PNGs were taken from that ChupathingyCE commit (by MrMilenko, "Draw
the HUD's text sheets from the restored art too"), and the way the pictures
are cut and placed follows its work there.

The other textures in this folder are not from this art: see `AUTHORS.md`.
