OpenH1 logo board, round 4

Hull Marking, revised: the 1 in signal orange, the H in sand. Three variations side by side: the hazard tab kept, the tab removed, and the tab removed with a brighter orange for small sizes.

Files are in website/logo/round4/, one folder per variation, made by website/logo/round4/build.py. Earlier rounds are in logo-board.html, logo-board-2.html and logo-board-3.html.

01

Hull Marking, the 1 in signal orange, tab kept

A: tab kept

Round 3's panel with the 1 in signal orange and the orange hazard tab kept beside the serial. The tab echoes the 1, but it is a second orange shape competing for the same attention.

Why it's safe. Unchanged from round 3: our own stencil letters on a generic chamfered panel; no emblem, insignia, unit name or lettering from the series.

Why it's not UNSC or Xbox. Not UNSC: no eagle, globe, wings or star, none of its type. Not Xbox: olive, sand and orange, no X, orb or green.

Why it's safe. Unchanged from round 3: our own stencil letters on a generic chamfered panel; no emblem, insignia, unit name or lettering from the series.

Why it's not UNSC or Xbox. Not UNSC: no eagle, globe, wings or star, none of its type. Not Xbox: olive, sand and orange, no X, orb or green.

Recommendation: C, brighter orange, no tab

Drop the tab. With the 1 in orange, the tab becomes a second orange shape of almost the same size in the bottom corner. It pulls the eye away from the 1 and makes the orange read as decoration rather than as the one signal on the panel. Without it (and without the seam that boxed it in) the bottom strip is a quiet sand serial, and the 1 owns the colour. At 16 pixels the tab was a stray orange bar under the letters; without it the small version is just H and 1.

Lift the orange. The round-3 orange (#ff6b1a) reads well large, but at 16 pixels its 2-pixel stem sinks a little next to the sand H. The small version's plate is now a shade darker for all three (#3a4232), which takes signal orange to about 3.7 to 1; the brighter tone (#ff8f2e) reaches about 4.6 to 1 (the sand H is 7.9), so the 1 holds its own in a browser tab while still reading as signal orange rather than amber. On light backgrounds the lockup uses a deeper orange (#c4500a, about 3.9 to 1 on the light ground) for the 1, so the wordmark keeps its contrast there too.

One colour. In single colour the 1 takes the H's colour; the stencil bridge that cuts its flag loose is what still marks it out, so the mark needs no second tone to work in print, embroidery or engraving.
