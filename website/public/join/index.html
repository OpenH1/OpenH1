Join yourfriend.

A friend invited you to play OpenH1. Open the invite in OpenH1 to join them.

Open in OpenH1

01 · Have OpenH1?

Paste it in.

If OpenH1 didn't open, copy this link:

  1. Start OpenH1. It joins a link it finds copied when it comes to the front.
  2. Or go to Multiplayer, then Join by Link.
  3. Or go to Multiplayer, then Party, and choose Paste Link.

The link works while your friend's copy of OpenH1 is running.

02 · Install OpenH1

Get it first.

Free, and always the latest build. Install it, open it once with your maps, then open the invite again.

Windows

For any Windows PC.

Download for Windows
  1. Unzip it anywhere, and open halo.exe.
  2. Windows may say it protected your PC: choose More info, then Run anyway.
Need your maps?

Steam Deck

And any 64-bit Linux PC with Vulkan.

Download for Steam Deck
  1. In Desktop Mode, download it and unzip it.
  2. Open install.sh in that folder, and choose your game's disc image or maps folder.
Need your maps?

Mac

Apple silicon, macOS 14.4 or later.

Download for Mac
  1. Open the downloaded .dmg.
  2. Drag OpenH1 to Applications, and open it from there.
Need your maps?

Android

64-bit Android 9 or later, with Vulkan 1.3.

Download for Android
  1. Download it on your phone, and open it.
  2. When Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then choose Install.
Need your maps?
